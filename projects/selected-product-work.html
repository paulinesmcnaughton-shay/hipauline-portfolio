Overview

A collection of smaller product design projects across mobility, e-commerce, mentorship, and future transportation.

These projects were shorter engagements or supporting product explorations, so they are grouped together as selected work rather than presented as individual case studies.

Across these projects, I contributed to mobile app flows, responsive web experiences, marketplace patterns, concept exploration, product structure, and interface design across different industries and product stages.

The projects

Mobility

Wheels

Urban mobility platform helping riders navigate cities through shared transportation, routing, and real-time ride experiences.

RoleProduct Designer
Year2020
PlatformiOS · Android

I contributed to mobile product design for rider-facing flows, focusing on map-based interactions, trip status, and key ride actions. The work supported a fast-moving transportation experience where users needed quick, clear decisions while on the go.

Key contributions
  • Rider mobile experience
  • Map and route-based interactions
  • Trip status and ride controls
  • Mobile UI patterns for quick actions

Supporting work across different product stages

These projects reflect my ability to move across different product contexts, from consumer mobility and e-commerce to early-stage platforms and future-facing transportation concepts.

While each engagement was smaller in scope, the work helped me build range across mobile, web, marketplace, and concept-driven product design.
