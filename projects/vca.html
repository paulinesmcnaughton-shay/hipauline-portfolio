Overview

The VCA desktop platform supported doctors, nurses, and clinic staff in managing hospital workflows, but the experience was primarily desktop-based and not optimized for moments when staff needed quick access to patient and visit information on the move.

The mobile concept explored how key clinical workflows could become more accessible in a fast-paced clinic environment while staying aligned with VCA’s existing systems.

Problem

The platform was mainly desktop-based, which restricted access during active workflows.
Staff needed faster access to patient information, orders, notes, appointments, and tasks.
Complex workflows were not optimized for mobile use.
Key actions required speed, clarity, and minimal friction in high-pressure environments.

Key insights

01

02

Solution and structure

Designed a streamlined mobile experience that mirrored core clinic workflows while simplifying complex actions and supporting faster task completion.

1

Patient list to profile

Move from a clinic-wide patient list straight into an individual patient profile.

2

Overview, medications, labs, notes, and orders

Tabbed sections that organize the full clinical picture without overwhelming the screen.

3

Quick actions

Fast paths for high-frequency tasks such as adding a note or creating an order.

4

Appointment review and scheduling access

Review visit details and reach scheduling without returning to a workstation.

5

Task and notification access

Stay on top of assigned tasks and clinic notifications throughout the day.
