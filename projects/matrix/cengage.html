Introduction

I contributed to the design of a zero-to-one AI-powered learning experience for Cengage, focused on helping students actively practice career-readiness skills instead of passively consuming static content.

The work explored simulations, personalized feedback, skill dashboards, and AI-assisted learning loops that could help students understand where they were improving and what to practice next.

Overview

Traditional learning platforms are often passive and assignment-driven. This project explored a next-generation AI-powered learning experience designed to increase engagement through simulations, real-time feedback, and personalized learning paths.

The goal was to help students practice career-readiness skills through interactive scenarios, skill tracking, and continuous feedback rather than relying only on static content.

Problem

Learning platforms often rely on static assignments, which can lead to lower engagement.
Students may consume content passively instead of actively practicing.
Personalized learning loops are limited, making it harder for students to understand what to improve next.
Students need clearer feedback that supports continuous skill development.

Role

Led the design of a zero-to-one AI-powered learning experience.
Partnered with product to support research with students and instructors.
Contributed to competitor analysis across learning platforms.
Designed MVP flows, interactions, and AI-driven learning experiences.

Key insights

01

Students learn best through active practice, not passive content.

02

Motivation increases when progress and feedback loops are clear.

03

Personalization is key to sustained engagement.

04

Learning experiences are often fragmented across too many tools.

Solution and structure

Designed an AI-powered learning loop built around five connected stages.

1

Dashboard

Tracks skills, progress, and learning gaps.

2

AI simulation

Generates real-world career-readiness scenarios.

3

Practice

Enables interactive conversations and applied learning.

4

Feedback

Provides scoring, insights, and guidance.

5

Repeat

Supports continuous improvement over time.

UX flow

1

Identify skill gaps

Surface what needs work through dashboard insights.

2

Generate simulations

Create scenarios based on the student's learning goals.

3

Engage in real-time

Practice through simulated, interactive conversation.

4

Receive feedback

Get structured feedback and scoring after each session.

5

Improve and repeat

Iterate on weaker areas to build skill over time.

UI design highlights

01

Skill dashboard

Visualizes skills, progress, and performance at a glance.

02

AI simulation builder

03

Conversational interface

A real-time interface for interactive, applied practice.

04

Feedback system

Scoring paired with actionable insights and next steps.

05

AI assistant support

Personalized learning recommendations throughout the loop.
