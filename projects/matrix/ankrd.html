ANKrD

AI-powered spending pause app helping people slow down impulse purchases, understand buying triggers, and make more intentional financial decisions.

RoleFounder · Designer · Builder
YearFeb 2026 — Present
PlatformiOS · Android

Overview

ANKrD started as a product concept around a simple behavioral question: what if the most helpful finance tool was not another budget tracker, but a moment of pause before you buy?

I designed ANKrD as a mobile product that helps users catch emotional spending patterns, reflect before purchases, and build healthier money habits through lightweight AI-guided check-ins.

The idea

Most personal finance apps focus on what happened after money is already spent. ANKrD focuses on the moment before the purchase.

The product creates a small pause between wanting something and buying it, helping users understand whether a purchase is useful, emotional, urgent, avoidant, or just a dopamine hit.

The goal is not to make people feel guilty for spending. The goal is to help them feel more in control.

Product goals

01

Create a pause before purchase

Give users a fast, low-friction way to check in before buying something.

02

Understand spending triggers

Help users recognize patterns like stress shopping, boredom, comparison, scarcity, or reward-based spending.

03

Make finance feel less shame-based

Avoid harsh budgeting language and make the product feel supportive, calm, and human.

04

Build behavior over time

Use streaks, reflection history, and purchase decisions to help users see progress.

05

Support smarter decisions, not perfect ones

The app should help users make better choices without making them feel like they failed.

The main design challenge was making a finance product feel supportive and emotionally intelligent without becoming too soft, too clinical, or too judgmental.

Key product flows

01

Pause before you buy

Users enter or capture something they want to buy, then move through a short reflection before deciding.

02

Name the trigger

The app helps users identify why they want the item, such as stress, boredom, urgency, reward, or actual need.

03

AI-guided reflection

ANKrD can ask a few lightweight questions to help users separate impulse from intention.

04

Decision moment

Users choose to buy, wait, skip, or save for later.

05

Track patterns over time

The product turns individual decisions into insight, helping users understand their spending behavior across time.

AI-assisted behavior support

ANKrD uses AI as a reflection layer, not as a financial advisor.

The AI experience is designed to help users slow down, ask better questions, and understand the emotional context behind a purchase.

This keeps the product focused on behavior change rather than hard financial advice, investment guidance, or strict budgeting.

Design decisions

Pause as the core interaction

The product is built around slowing down the purchase moment instead of reviewing spending after the fact.

Supportive, not scolding

The tone avoids guilt, shame, and aggressive budgeting language.

Behavior first, numbers second

ANKrD focuses on triggers, decisions, and patterns before charts or financial dashboards.

Lightweight check-ins

The flow needs to feel fast enough to use in real life, especially when someone is already tempted to buy.

Premium but calm visual system

The design direction uses a more mature, minimal interface so the product feels trustworthy without feeling like a bank app.

Current status

ANKrD is currently in product concept and prototype development.

The core direction, product loop, onboarding, pause flow, trigger system, and AI reflection model are being shaped as part of the MVP.

The next step is refining the decision flow, testing the tone of the AI prompts, and building out the product's behavior insights.

What this project shows

ANKrD shows how I approach early-stage product strategy, especially when the problem is emotional, behavioral, and hard to solve with traditional dashboards.

It also shows how I think about AI in product design: not as a flashy feature, but as a way to create more personal, reflective, and useful user experiences.

A behavior design product, still evolving

ANKrD is still in progress, but the core product direction is clear: help people pause before they buy, understand their spending triggers, and make more intentional decisions without shame.
